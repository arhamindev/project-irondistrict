QUICK ANSWERS

Ask on WhatsApp
Do I need to sign a long contract?

No. Memberships are billed monthly, and you can pause or cancel with 7 days' notice.

I'm a beginner. Is this gym right for me?

Yes. Every new member gets a short walkthrough of the floor and equipment, and our coaches can point you to the right program to start.

Can I visit before I join?

Of course. Choose "Facility visit" in the inquiry form, or message us on WhatsApp, and we'll arrange a time for a free walkthrough.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM until 4:00 AM the next morning. We're closed on Sundays.

Is personal training included?

Premium includes a personal training consultation. One-to-one sessions can be added to any plan, so ask us about coaching when you enquire.

How do I join?

Send an inquiry below. We'll reply on WhatsApp with next steps, and you can complete your registration at the front desk.
